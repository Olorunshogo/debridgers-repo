## CTO-KPI-Summary

Notes on what's deliberately excluded from `docs/jottings/CTO-KPI-SUMMARY.md`
(the CEO-facing scorecard), and why:

- **Uptime SLA (e.g. 99.9%):** premature at current infrastructure scale -
  reported as "zero P0 incidents" instead until there's a real on-call and
  redundancy setup to back a formal SLA.
- **Multi-year vision targets** (200+ agents, 10-city presence, mobile app):
  these are 5-10 year company targets, not this quarter's CTO scorecard.
  They stay in the onboarding doc as direction, not there as a grading bar.
- **Feature-by-feature backlog detail:** tracked internally in TASKS.md/
  PLAN.md; not useful at that altitude.
