## 1. Company Overview

Debridgers LTD is a Kaduna-based agricultural produce and food distribution company. We source staple food commodities - currently rice, beans, and cooking oil, with maize, groundnut, and soya beans in development - and deliver them to business and institutional buyers across Kaduna metropolis, at prices set against the open market rather than negotiated case by case.

The company is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission under RC Number 9573579, holds a Tax Identification Number with the Nigeria Revenue Service, and is registered with the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML) under the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission. We are an early-stage, founder-led business: self-funded, currently operating in a single city, with a small but genuine and repeating customer base built entirely through direct outreach rather than advertising spend.

We describe our stage honestly, because we think a partner should be able to trust every figure in this profile at face value. We are not a large company. We are a disciplined one, and the rest of this document is intended to show that discipline rather than assert it.

## 2. Vision and Mission

**Our vision** is to become the most trusted and innovative agricultural supply company in our market, owning every step of the process from the farm to final delivery, built on technology, integrity, and operational excellence.

**Our mission** is to source and deliver essential food commodities directly from trusted local farmers to the households, businesses, and institutions that depend on them, building toward a supply chain we understand and control end to end rather than one we merely pass through.

We summarise what we actually do, today, in one sentence: **Debridgers replaces opaque, uncoordinated intermediation with transparent, measurable, and lower-cost distribution.** We do not claim to eliminate the intermediaries who already serve Nigeria's food supply chain - aggregators, transporters, financiers, and graders provide real, necessary services. Our claim is narrower and, we believe, stronger: that the connection between farmer and buyer can be made more transparent, better coordinated, and more reliable than it currently is, and that this is worth building deliberately.

## 3. The Problem We Address

Nigerian agricultural supply chains are widely fragmented. Farmers frequently lack organised, predictable access to buyers who will take their produce reliably and at a fair price. Businesses that need consistent volumes of staple commodities - restaurants, caterers, institutions - frequently cannot source them predictably, and instead absorb price volatility, send staff to negotiate in open markets, and carry the risk of inconsistent quality with no recourse.

Debridgers exists to close that gap from the demand side: we source commodities into a small, disciplined operation and sell them to businesses at a fixed, published price, with a guarantee that a defective delivery will be replaced without argument. Our recent engagement with KADA sharpened our own understanding of where our real value lies - not in finding farmers, which the state's agricultural development infrastructure already does at scale, but in the structured, disciplined work of turning available supply into reliable commercial outcomes for buyers who need it.

## 4. How We Operate

Our operating model is straightforward and, we believe, unusually disciplined for a company at our stage:

**Sourcing.** We source staple commodities from established suppliers into a small warehouse facility, currently housed within existing premises made available by one of our founders. Every commodity we sell is sold exactly as received from the supplier, with the original seal intact - we do not open, re-portion, or repackage. This keeps our operation simple, keeps our costs low, and keeps our quality guarantee straightforward to honour, since an unopened seal is itself evidence that a buyer received exactly what the supplier sent.

**Sales.** Our sales to date have come entirely from direct outreach - WhatsApp, phone calls, and personal visits by our founding team to food-service businesses. We also operate a web ordering platform, though it has not yet generated an order independently of that direct outreach. We consider this an honest and important fact about our current stage: Debridgers today is a field sales operation supported by strong technology, not yet a platform business, and we are candid with ourselves and with partners about that distinction.

**Payment and delivery.** Our standard practice is that a customer pays before we deliver, and we only ever process a confirmed, paid order. This means we do not carry inventory risk against a sale that has not happened, and it is the single most attractive structural feature of our business: the customer, in effect, finances the trade. As we work to win buyers away from existing suppliers, there are cases where we will deliver first and collect payment on delivery instead, as a deliberate and carefully bounded exception rather than our standing practice - every order we have processed to date has, in fact, been paid in advance.

**Fulfilment.** Once an order is confirmed, we dispatch stock from our warehouse and deliver by hired vehicle, with delivery priced by zone to reflect the actual cost of the trip.

## 6. Our Customers

Our primary and proven customer segment is B2B food-service businesses - restaurants and caterers who need consistent volumes of staples at a price they can plan around. We have identified, and are actively developing, a second segment of institutional and processing buyers, including universities, employers, residential communities, and, through our KADA engagement, compound feed producers requiring raw materials at scale. Further segments - market traders and shops, and individual households - are recognised opportunities we have deliberately deferred, so that our small team's attention stays on the segments where we have already proven demand.

We are also building two additional participant groups into our model: **field agents**, who will sell on a commission basis within their own neighbourhoods once recruited, and **farmers and suppliers**, whom we currently work with as established commercial suppliers and intend to work with more directly over time - the outgrower pilot proposed in our accompanying concept note is the first structured step in that direction.

## 7. What We Offer Each Group We Serve

**To buyers:** a published, market-based price with no haggling; a quality guarantee with no-questions-asked replacement for a defective delivery; the convenience of ordering by WhatsApp, phone, or our web platform; and fees stated up front, calculated identically whether a buyer requests a quote or checks out directly. We believe our strongest differentiator is not price alone, but price certainty combined with a guaranteed replacement - a business that knows exactly what it will pay, receives its order reliably, and knows a defect will be corrected without an argument, has been given something the informal market does not offer as a package.

**To future field agents:** the ability to sell without needing capital of their own, weekly payouts, the opportunity to build a customer base in their own neighbourhood, free training, and a growth path into higher responsibility as the agent network matures.

**To suppliers and, in time, farmers directly:** reliable, repeat purchasing rather than one-off transactions, clear and recorded payment terms, and - as our direct farmer relationships develop - market access without needing to go through an additional layer of intermediation.

## 8. Pricing Approach

We sell goods at market reference price and earn our margin from disciplined procurement, not from marking up the customer. Delivery is priced to recover its actual cost, by zone, rather than to generate profit - for example, a two-package delivery within Kaduna South is priced at ₦4,000, reflecting the measured cost of that trip, with a modest additional charge per extra package and a cap per delivery. A separate, small service fee covers the cost of payment processing, order administration, reconciliation, and our replacement guarantee, again set to recover cost rather than to profit from it. We believe this separation - profit from goods, cost recovery on everything else - is what allows us to offer a fixed, honest price rather than a marked-up one.

## 9. Technology and Verified Systems

Debridgers has built and operates a full commerce and operations platform: buyer registration and catalogue browsing, a shopping cart that stays synchronised across devices, card and bank-transfer checkout with server-side price verification, an agent management system covering applications, approvals, identity verification, stock requests, commissions, and payouts, and administrative dashboards for managing the business day to day.

We consider our payment integrity to be one of our strongest assets. Every transaction is tracked to the exact kobo, reconciled against our payment processor, and verified through more than seventy automated tests covering payment confirmation, wallet accuracy, and account isolation. Our live payment integration has completed real transactions successfully. For any partnership involving the movement of value between parties - as in an input-credit arrangement with farmers - we believe this kind of verified, auditable record-keeping is not a convenience but a requirement, and it is one we already meet.

## 12. Financial Position and Working Capital

Debridgers currently holds approximately ₦300,000 in working capital. Because our standard practice is that customers pay before we deliver, this capital does not need to fund inventory in the way a typical distribution business's would - it functions instead as a buffer, covering short settlement gaps, up-front transport costs, and the occasional replacement under our quality guarantee. We consider this negative working capital structure - in which the customer's payment effectively funds the purchase of the goods they are buying - the single strongest structural feature of our financial model.

The limit of this structure is honest and worth stating: institutional buyers who require payment on delivery or on standard commercial terms cannot currently be served under a prepayment-only policy. Where that limitation matters, the right answer is a modest, well-structured credit facility sized against a formula we can demonstrate from our own order history, rather than an open-ended need for capital - and it is not something we are asking KADA to provide, as set out in our accompanying concept note.

## 13. Governance, Compliance, and Risk Management

Debridgers operates as a distributor: we sell products exactly as received from our suppliers, which keeps us on the more straightforward side of Nigeria's food-safety regulatory framework. We intend to become a packager in future - repackaging bulk commodities into smaller units - and we are committed to obtaining the appropriate regulatory guidance before that transition begins, rather than after. We hold personal data, banking details, and identity verification documents for our team and prospective agents, and we take our obligations under Nigeria's data protection regulation seriously and already applicable to our current operations.

On financial compliance specifically: Debridgers is registered with the Special Control Unit Against Money Laundering (SCUML) under the EFCC, in addition to holding both our CAC registration and Nigeria Revenue Service Tax Identification Number. We consider this an important, and deliberately front-loaded, part of being a credible partner for any arrangement - like the outgrower pilot proposed separately - that involves the structured movement of inputs, produce, and money between multiple parties.

We manage operational risk candidly rather than by omission. Our current risks include customer concentration, given our small existing customer base; the demands a small team faces in simultaneously building technology, sourcing, and selling; and, as we grow into farmer-facing programmes such as the outgrower pilot proposed separately, the price-risk inherent in any arrangement that fixes input distribution before a harvest price is known. We believe naming these risks plainly, rather than presenting only a favourable picture, is itself part of what makes Debridgers a credible partner.

## 14. Our Growth Plan

Over the coming months, our priorities are to measure our business precisely rather than estimate it, to grow our repeat business customer base meaningfully beyond its current small size, to deepen our supplier relationships for our core products, and to responsibly begin recruiting field agents once our systems and financial controls are proven ready to support them. Over the longer term, our ambition is to become a trusted farm-to-door distribution platform across Northern Nigeria, building direct relationships with farmers, cooperative bodies, and state agricultural institutions, and eventually operating across multiple cities with a data-informed, technology-supported supply chain that benefits farmers and buyers alike.

We pursue this growth deliberately. We do not expand to a new city, a new product line, or a new operating model until the current one is proven to sustain itself - we believe this discipline, more than any single feature of our platform, is what will make Debridgers durable.
